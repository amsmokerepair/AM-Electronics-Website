Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Quick answers to what customers ask us every day.

How long does an iPhone screen repair take?

Most iPhone screen replacements are completed in 30 minutes or less while you wait. We carry common models in stock — if yours is less common, we'll tell you upfront.

Is the diagnostic really free?

100% free, zero obligation. We inspect your device, explain what's wrong, give you the price — and you decide. No charge either way.

What warranty do you give on repairs?

Every repair includes a 90-day warranty on both parts and labor. Same issue comes back within 90 days? We fix it free, no arguments.

Do you use original (OEM) parts?

We use OEM-grade parts — same quality as manufacturer components — for most repairs. We'll always tell you the exact part quality before we start.

Can you fix my PS5 or Xbox Series X?

Yes. We repair PS4, PS5, Xbox One, Xbox Series X/S, and Nintendo Switch. HDMI ports, disc drives, overheating, Joy-Con drift, power issues — we handle it all.

Do I need an appointment?

No appointment needed — walk in Monday through Saturday, 10AM to 8PM. You can also call ahead and we'll give you a phone quote before you leave the house.

How do your prices compare to uBreakiFix or CPR?

We're typically 20–40% cheaper than chain repair stores. We're locally owned with lower overhead, and we pass those savings directly to you. Call us to compare.

Can you recover data from a broken device?

Often yes. We regularly recover data from water-damaged phones, failed hard drives, and devices with broken screens. Bring it in for a free assessment.
