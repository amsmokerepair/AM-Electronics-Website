Common questions

How much does iPhone screen repair cost?

iPhone screen replacement at A&M Electronics Repair starts at $74 for an iPhone 6–8, from $99 for an iPhone 13, from $109 for an iPhone 14 and from $179 for an iPhone 17 Pro Max. See the full price list at am-electronicsrepair.org/prices.

How long does an iPhone screen repair take?

Most iPhone screen replacements take about 30 minutes while you wait.

Is the diagnosis free?

Usually, yes. We inspect the device, explain what's wrong and give you the price. Most devices are checked free. If yours needs bench time to find the problem, the diagnostic is $40, and that $40 goes toward your repair.

What warranty do you give?

Every repair includes a 30-day warranty on parts and labor.

Do you fix PS5, Xbox and Nintendo Switch?

Yes. PS5 and PS4 HDMI ports are $170 same-day or $120 in 2–5 days. Xbox HDMI ports are $170 same-day or $130 in 2–5 days. Joy-Con drift is $40 per stick.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk in Monday through Saturday, 10 AM to 8 PM. We're closed Sunday.

Where is A&M Electronics Repair?

14218 AR-107, Jacksonville, AR 72076. That's in Gravel Ridge, about 5 minutes from Sherwood, 14 minutes from North Little Rock and Little Rock Air Force Base, and 18 minutes from Little Rock.

What parts do you use?

Quality aftermarket parts by default — LCD screens on standard iPhones and OLED screens on Pro models — and we tell you the exact part before we start.

How do your prices compare to uBreakiFix or CPR?

We're typically 20–40% cheaper than chain repair stores. We're locally owned with lower overhead.
